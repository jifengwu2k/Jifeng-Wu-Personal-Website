Purpose
-------

This document is meant to be printed and placed in visible locations so that, if I suddenly die or become incapacitated, trusted people can act promptly, calmly, and in good faith.

Actions
-------

1. Notify relevant people
  - Family
  - Close friends
  - Collaborators
  - Employers or institutions
2. Obtain access to relevant platforms and devices
  - rsync.net
  - Mobile phone
  - Laptop
  - Desktop
  - Other personal computing devices
3. Read `quipu/` (on rsync.net and the terminals of personal computing devices)
  - Identify current projects
  - Identify unfinished obligations
  - Identify information needed for handoff or closure
4. Read `secrets/` (on rsync.net and the terminals of personal computing devices)
  - Find instructions for important accounts
  - Access passwords, keys, and other materials needed for continuity
